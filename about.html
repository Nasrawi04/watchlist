About MyScreenFolio

It started with one question: “What’s your favourite show?”

MyScreenFolio is made by two friends and university students, Mohammad and Narimaan. It’s the place we wanted and couldn’t find: everything we watch — movies, TV shows, anime and cartoons — in one folio, rated properly.

“We could have used the apps that already exist. But one was only for movies, another only for shows. We wanted to see everything — and rate it on what actually makes it good.”— Mohammad & Narimaan
On this page

    Our story

    1. A question we couldn’t answer

      It began with the two of us trying to work out our favourite shows — a ranking. Neither of us could. “I like this, I like that…” — we were just naming random titles.

    2. Notes apps and a Word document

      We were always telling each other “you should watch this”, “you have to see that”, and writing it all down — one of us in a notes app, the other in a Word document. It was a mess.

    3. “Why not build it?”

      The idea was Narimaan’s; Mohammad could code it. So he built something — extremely basic. Narimaan took one look and had a list of ideas. Mohammad kept coding them.

    4. Idea after idea

      We both had big ideas, and we kept building on them, and building on them. Today we both code and go over everything together.

    5. Six or seven months later

      MyScreenFolio has been our passion project for the last six or seven months, and it’s grown into something we’re genuinely proud of. We’re not stopping any time soon.

    What makes MSF different

    Everything, in one place

    Other apps pick one: Letterboxd is for movies, Serializd for shows. MSF keeps movies, TV shows, anime and cartoons together — one library, one profile, one set of stats.

    Ratings with depth

    A single star score can’t say why. MSF rates the core things that make any piece of art work — Narrative & Storytelling, Characters & Performances, Visual & Technical Craft, Overall Experience (and Animation Quality for anime and cartoons) — plus bonus criteria when they matter. Your score is built from them.

    Made for watching together

    It began as two friends recommending things to each other, so sharing is built in: taste matches, Compare, Blend, Movie Night, Challenges, Polls and Episode Chat.

    Yours, and private when you want

    No ads, no selling your data, no trackers. Messages are end-to-end encrypted, and you decide who sees each title, list and note. How we handle your data.
