    What helps us help you

    • Your username — and write from the email on your account if it’s about your account or your data, so we know it’s you.
    • For a bug: the page, what you did, what you expected and what happened instead; your device and browser (for example “iPhone, Safari”); a screenshot if you can.
    • Never send us your password, your Messages recovery code or your passcode. We will never ask for them.
