    1. Cookies and browser storage

    Cookies are small pieces of text a website saves in your browser. Websites can also save information in other parts of your browser, and data protection law treats these the same way, so this policy covers all of them:

    • Local storage — kept until it’s deleted (by MSF, by you, or by your browser).
    • Session storage — kept only until you close the tab.
    • IndexedDB — a small database in your browser.
    • Cache storage — copies of files, used by MSF’s “service worker” so the app opens quickly and works offline.

    Everything MSF saves stays on your device and is only read by MSF. It isn’t used to track you across other websites.

    7. Extra sources (optional)

    These save nothing on your device, but your browser contacts another service, which sees your IP address and browser like any website would:

    • AniList (graphql.anilist.co) — when you search, MSF also asks AniList for matching anime and their genres. If you say no: anime still shows up from TMDB, just with fewer extra results.
    • wsrv.nl — a free image helper used when an exported episode chart needs a poster that can’t be drawn directly. If you say no: that image is made without the poster.

    Importing from AniList (Settings › Data › Import) always contacts AniList, because you ask it to.

    8. Other websites’ cookies

    MSF doesn’t put advertising, analytics or social-media code on its pages, so no other company sets cookies through MSF. Posters and photos load from TMDB’s and Cloudinary’s image servers, and the site’s files from GitHub Pages — they see your IP address when they send a file, but don’t set cookies for MSF. When you tap a link to YouTube, IMDb, Instagram or a streaming service, you’re on their site and their cookie policy applies.

    9. Your browser’s controls

    You can also delete or block storage in your browser’s settings — for example Safari › Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data, or Chrome / Edge › Settings › Privacy › Site data. Blocking necessary storage signs you out and stops parts of MSF working. Clearing site data also resets your cookie choice, so you’ll be asked again.

    10. Changes

    Whenever MSF starts storing something new on your device, it’s added to this page in the same release. If the kinds themselves change, or what they mean, everyone is asked to choose again.
